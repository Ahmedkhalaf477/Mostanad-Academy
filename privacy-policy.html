Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Mostanad Academy ("Mostanad Academy", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mostanad Academy may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website, educational services, and AI-powered tools and integrations, including Nada AI.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Name and basic profile information.
  • Email address and contact information.
  • Information voluntarily provided when communicating with Mostanad Academy.
  • LinkedIn profile information when you authorize our application to access LinkedIn.
  • Information necessary to publish content to a LinkedIn member account or LinkedIn Page when you explicitly authorize such functionality.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and usage information.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve our services.

2. LinkedIn Integration

Mostanad Academy may provide integration with LinkedIn through Nada AI.

When you authorize our LinkedIn integration, we may access information and permissions provided by LinkedIn according to the authorization screen and permissions granted to our application.

Depending on the permissions granted, the integration may be used to:

  • Authenticate the user.
  • Access basic LinkedIn account information.
  • Create and publish content on behalf of an authorized LinkedIn member.
  • Create and publish content on behalf of an authorized LinkedIn Page or organization where the required permissions and administrative authorization are available.
Important: LinkedIn access is used only after the user explicitly authorizes the relevant permissions through LinkedIn.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Mostanad Academy services.
  • Provide Nada AI functionality.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Create, review, manage, and publish educational content when authorized by the user.
  • Communicate with users about our services.
  • Improve the performance, reliability, and security of our services.
  • Prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or abuse.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication information, integration data, and access credentials are intended to be stored securely and accessed only when necessary for the operation of the authorized service.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be processed or transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide functionality explicitly requested or authorized by the user, including platform integrations such as LinkedIn.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or services.

6. LinkedIn Data

Information obtained through LinkedIn is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and the functionality presented during authorization.

We do not use LinkedIn information for unrelated purposes.

Users may revoke access to our application through the relevant LinkedIn account settings. After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to access LinkedIn resources through that authorization.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain legitimate business records, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Users may contact us to request deletion of information associated with their use of our services, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

Our services may interact with third-party platforms and services, including LinkedIn and infrastructure or AI services used to operate Nada AI.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services they choose to connect to our services.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Request information about how your data is used.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information below.
